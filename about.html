Our Story

Born from a broken healthcare experience

Stackly started with a simple observation: insurance was doing the opposite of protecting people. Confusing policies, slow claims, and fine print that only ever helped the insurer.

So we rebuilt it from the ground up — pairing a nationwide cashless network with AI that processes claims in minutes, not months. Today, millions of families sleep easier knowing Stackly has their back.

The Problem

Slow, opaque, and stressful insurance that failed when it mattered most.

The Solution

Fast, transparent, human coverage — delivered through intelligent technology.
